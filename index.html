Our Philosophy

Guidance, not gatekeeping.

We started Fourth Avenue Education because we remembered what it felt like to sit where our students sit now — unsure which parts of the process actually mattered, and which were noise. Our approach is built on three convictions.

Individualized advising

Every student arrives with a different transcript, a different voice, and a different definition of success. We build a plan around the student in front of us, not a template built for the average applicant.

Ethical guidance

We coach, edit, and question — we do not write. Every essay, activity list, and application a student submits through our guidance is theirs, in their own words, built on their own experience.

Authentic storytelling

The strongest applications aren't the most impressive on paper — they're the most honest. Our work is helping students notice what's genuinely compelling about their own story and say it clearly.

How We Work

Why Families Choose Us

    Our founders applied to selective programs in the last few admissions cycles — the process is fresh, not theoretical.

  • Two advisors, one student

    Strategy and writing are handled by specialists who communicate directly with each other about your student.

  • Ethical, essay-coaching only

    We will never write an essay or fabricate an achievement. Every word submitted is the student's own.

  • A plan that fits the student

    From a four-year roadmap for a freshman to a 24-hour turnaround before a deadline, packages flex to real needs.
